# --- Refraction carriers (M2_REFRACTION_DESIGN.md) ---
# A refraction emitter draws into the distortion buffer, not into the scene, so
# it is absent from the transparent histogram by construction and only the
# Refraction trace slot sees it. The three auras carry the strong masks that
# actually bend pixels; `instancenewportal_blue` carries a deliberately faint
# one (peak colour 10/255), which is what pins the magnitude rather than just
# the presence. `8fx_generic_water_missile` is the BC5 arm: its distortion
# texture is a BLP with alphaType 11, the one format the parser used to reject
# outright, and a regression there shows up as a flat mask and nothing else.
spells/cfx_paladin_divineshield_statebase.m2
spells/cfx_paladin_dawnlight_aura.m2
spells/11fx_manavent_aura.m2
spells/instancenewportal_blue.m2
spells/8fx_generic_water_missile.m2
